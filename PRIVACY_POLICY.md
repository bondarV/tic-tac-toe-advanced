## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Tic-Tac-Toe application ("the App") handles user data. By using the App, you agree
to the terms outlined in this document.

## 2. Data Processing Information (Client-Side Only)

The app code runs on the client side (in your browser) and stores gameplay/settings data locally on your device. The
app itself does not send gameplay or profile data to backend servers. To load the app in your browser, standard network
requests to hosting infrastructure (for example, a CDN) still occur, and that infrastructure may process basic access
logs:

* **No Application Tracking:** The App itself does not collect, transmit, or store personal or gameplay data on external
  servers. However, please note that the hosting provider or CDN serving the application may automatically collect and
  log standard request metadata (such as your IP address and browser user agent) as part of basic web infrastructure.
* **Local Storage Usage:** The App uses your browser's `localStorage` to save game history, game settings, and theme
  preferences so they persist between sessions on your device. The current version does not include a separate consent
  banner or an in-app consent toggle for storage. No third-party tracking or analytical services are used.

## 3. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete this data at any time by explicitly clearing the site's
storage or site data for this domain. This can typically be found in your browser's privacy/security settings or site
data settings (e.g., "Clear site data" or "Manage site data"). Clearing your standard browser cache may not remove
`localStorage` data.
